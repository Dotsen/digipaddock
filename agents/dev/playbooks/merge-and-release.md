# Merge, release, deploy and rollout

Read this when the owner says «мержи» or «мержи и выкати» in the thread, or the cabinet wakes you with his signed approval to merge (and release) — and after the merge until the card ends: the deploy of the merged commit and the rollout of its sheet, and any server step the merged work needs. «Workflow» and step numbers refer to the Workflow section of your main instruction.

## `kd-merge`

- `kd-merge <project> <N> <sha> [--release]` — merge the PR (and deploy `main` to {{host.stage_alias}} with `--release`; that waits up to ~8 minutes for the deploy, never for a reinstall: a release that leaves generated agents outdated asks the factory itself and prints `установка ролей a,b: операция <id> — закончи ход: kd-work finish KD-<n> --outcome wait_dependency --operation <id> --note "…"` — end the turn exactly so, the operation's end wakes you; `установка ролей a,b: запрос release:<rev> уйдёт после обновления кабинета — … --operation release:<rev> …` — the same, on the request's key (the release asks once the cabinet runs the new code); `шаг <component> <mode>: операция <id>` — a component step approved on `"revision": "release"` the release requested (KD-9121): the printed `kd-work finish` names every operation, `--operation <id> --operation <id>`, copy it whole; `шаг … не запрошен: …` — tell the owner; `роли … не переустановлены: …` — no approved factory step on this card covers them: `kd-factory plan` (playbooks/factory.md), or tell the owner). `sha` is the full head hash, 40 characters, from `kd-forge pr`. Forgejo merges only a PR the owner approved on exactly this head — signed in the cabinet (the Approve comes from kd-review) or with Approve in Forgejo; a new commit dismisses his approval, and without it `kd-merge` refuses («PR не одобрен владельцем …»). There is no Claude Code card. Call it **only after the owner's word**: his «мержи» / «мержи и выкати» in the thread, or the cabinet's wake `[board] KD-<n>: the owner approved PR #<N> on <sha7> in the cabinet and asks to merge it …` (`… to merge and release it` — with `--release`); a wake that says `approve only` is no word to merge. `--release` takes only his signature with the release (or his own Approve in Forgejo): on a merge signature it refuses («выкатка не подписана …») — merge without `--release`, or wait for his new signature. «не одобрен» — do not retry; wait for his text. A passport that wants the branch current with its target refuses a PR that is behind it (`base_moved: целевая ветка <target> ушла вперёд …`): in the card's worktree `git fetch origin && git merge origin/<target>`, the affected checks, `kd-pr`, `kd-forge check` where the project has required checks, and `kd-forge publish` again. A merge of the target with no conflict and no commit of yours since his signature keeps it: publish says `подпись владельца перенесена с <sha7> …` (kd-review checked that the new head is the signed one updated from the target and the PR's own diff is the same) and, for a signature to merge, prints the `kd-merge` line of the mode he signed — run it on the new head, his word stands. Anything else — a conflict you resolved, a commit of yours, a cut diff — says `подпись не перенесена (…)`: one line to the owner, the new head needs his approval again. If the owner merged in the web himself and says «выкати», the same `kd-merge … --release` releases the merged commit. The merge, the release and a refusal also reach the owner as a notice in the thread of the card your PR title names (`KD-<n>: …`).

## `kd-forge status`

- `kd-forge status <project> <sha>` — the checks of one commit by its hash (7–40 hex characters), the merged one too: `коммит <sha12>: N проверок`, then one `<state> <context>` line per check (`success`, `failure`, `pending`, …), or `проверок нет`. After a merge it shows the deploy the push to the target branch started (Workflow 8). A read: it posts nothing.

## Workflow step 8 — the owner's word to merge

8. Owner says «мержи» (or «мержи и выкати»), or the cabinet wakes you with his signed approval to merge (and release): `kd-forge pr <project> <N>` → the full head sha → `kd-merge <project> <N> <sha40>` (+ `--release`). Without his approval on that head `kd-merge` refuses: tell him in one line «нужно одобрение PR в кабинете на <sha7>» and end the turn with `--outcome wait_user`. On success: from the clone, `git worktree remove ../<Repo>.KD-<n>`; `kd-task edit KD-<n> --append-notes "merged <sha7>[, released <rev>]"`, a line in `memory/tasks/KD-<n>.md` (a lasting pitfall — `kd-notes add`), then the deploy and the rollout (the bullets below) where the project has them, and only after them end the turn with `kd-work finish KD-<n> --outcome done --note "merged <sha7>[, released <rev>]"` — on a project where you may not close a card it refuses: `--outcome review`. If the release printed an operation, end the turn with `--outcome wait_dependency --operation <id>` instead and finish `done` on the turn its end wakes you (a failed reinstall: tell the owner what failed). A check that is only due later (a live check the card wants a day after the release, a window the owner named) — `kd-work finish KD-<n> --outcome wait_until --until <moment with its time zone> --note "…"`, up to 7 days: the cabinet wakes you then; never ask the owner to write to you at a time. Remind the owner in the same message if the cage is not empty: generated agents are reinstalled through the factory (`kd-factory`, or the release's operation), a catalog component through `kd-factory install` under the owner's signed plan; other units and installers are the owner's to run.

## The deploy and the rollout

- **The deploy and the rollout are part of the card.** Where a push to the target branch starts a deploy (the repository carries its workflow), a card is not done at the merge — whatever `## Rollout` says: after every `kd-merge` there, `git fetch origin` in the clone, the merged commit is `git log -1 --format=%H origin/<target>`, and `kd-forge status <project> <that sha>` shows the deploy its push started. The server steps of `## Rollout` are never the owner's to run: handoff is to the sysadmin.
- A project whose environments the «Deploys» page lists (`alert`, `api`, `arm`, `keeta`): do not poll — end the turn with `kd-work finish KD-<n> --outcome wait_dependency --deploy <project>:<env> --note "<waiting for the <env> deploy, in the owner’s language>"`, the environment the merge deploys to (`api:staging`, `alert:test`; a prod run only after the owner's word for prod). It waits for a finished run of that environment holding your last merged PR (`@<sha>` names another commit): `what you waited for is resolved` — the run passed, go on as after a green deploy; `ended without the answer you needed (deploy <project>:<env> failed: run #N <log>)` — it failed, as `failure` below, the log is the run's.
- `failure` — the deploy did not pass: read what the check names, fix it on the same card with a new PR (steps 4–8); no rollout request on a red deploy. Elsewhere — `pending`, or `проверок нет` right after the merge — `kd-work finish KD-<n> --outcome wait_until --until <a few minutes ahead, with its time zone> --note "<waiting for deploy <sha7>, in the owner’s language>"`, and look again on that wake; `проверок нет` on that wake too — the push started no check: say so in the note and go on as after a green one.
- Green, and `## Rollout` says no additional server steps are needed: finish the card as above.
- Green, and the sheet has steps for an environment: put that environment's sheet into the card — `kd-task edit KD-<n> --append-notes "<steps, checks, rollback>"` (the sysadmin cannot read a PR body) — then `kd-work assist request KD-<n> --kind server_change --to @sysadmin --step "Rollout of <project> <environment>. See the sheet in the card notes." --key rollout-<environment>` and end the turn with `kd-work finish KD-<n> --outcome wait_dependency --assist <id> --note "…"`. One request per environment.
- The answer carries the results (`KD-<m>: …`): the environment is a test stand, where a rollout sheet runs without the owner — the cabinet approved the sysadmin's plan by rule and the steps ran in its turn. No Blocked, no line to the owner: check the card's acceptance criteria against what each step showed (a one-time link the sheet asked for is in the answer; the HTTP checks with it are yours), then finish the card as above. A `failed` answer names the step and what it printed: what is yours to fix goes into a new PR on the same card.
- The answer names the sysadmin's card (`план на KD-<m>` — any other environment): `kd-task edit KD-<n> --status Blocked --dep KD-<m> --comment "waits for the rollout KD-<m>"` (`--dep` replaces the list: keep the existing dependencies), tell the owner in one line, in his language, that the <environment> rollout plan on KD-<m> awaits his signature, then `kd-work finish KD-<n> --outcome wait_dependency --card KD-<m> --note "…"`. Its Done wakes you: `kd-task edit KD-<n> --status "In Progress"`, check the card's acceptance criteria against what the sheet's checks showed (the result of KD-<m>; a read you still need is `server_read` help), then finish the card as above.
- A criterion that names a stand is checked on the stand before the card goes to Review, and the checks are yours: what an HTTP request answers — the health URL, a page, a sign-in kept in a cookie jar — you ask yourself through your exit, with a script with `requests` from the worktree (`curl` is denied to you); a file, a log or a database of the server (MySQL, a stand's SQLite file) — `server_read` help; a tool of the application on the server (a one-time link, a test user's switch) — a step of the rollout sheet. The owner gets only what needs his own hands — a real device, his own account — named by criterion, never a table of checks to click through.
- `failed --reason capability_missing` — the catalog has no such environment, tool, switch section or unit: adding it is a PR in `digipaddock-deploy` (`apps` of `agents/sysadmin/operations.json`) on this card, approved like any other, and the request is repeated after its release with a new `--key`. A step no catalog entry can express — free SQL, a shell script, a migration the deploy does not apply — is not a rollout step: the project's own deploy or tool must do it; fix that in the project.
- A production environment: ask for its rollout only after the owner's word for prod in the thread, as a request of its own, and say in the step that he asked.

## Before you ask the owner — a server step your merged work needs

A fact about a service, a version or a log is yours to find (`kd-host-read` on the agents' host — a unit's journal and state, platform files; `kd-diag`, the code in your clone, the card's thread) or, beyond those reads (a managed server, the monitor's state), @sysadmin's: ask for it as help on your approved card (the common rules, «A step of another authority»), not with a handoff — never the owner's to fetch by running a command for you. The same holds for a change on a server your merged work needs (the project's own tool, a switch, a reload): it is @sysadmin's to plan and the owner's only to sign (on a test stand not even that: the cabinet approves such a plan by rule) — ask for it as `server_change` help (Workflow 8), never as a list of commands for the owner.
