# Attention events

`kd-boss-board attention-list` prints one line per open event: `<id> <state> <level> <rule> <subject> revision <…> since <time>`. The subject is a card (`KD-<n>`). Handle each event, then ack it with what you did and when to look again:

`kd-boss-board attention-ack --event <id> --action <ref> --next-check <minutes> --reason "<one line>"`

| `--action` | When | `--next-check` |
|---|---|---|
| `assist:<id>`, `decision:<id>`, `wait:<id>`, `job:<id>` | the event waits for that help, decision, wait or worker job | 5–1440 |
| `card:KD-<m>` | it waits for another card | 5–1440 |
| `owner` | you asked the owner in the chat (`kd-task chat --in-chat --post`); the reason says what | 5–1440 |
| `directive:<id>` | you gave the card's worker a directive | 5–30 |
| `edit:<revision>` | you changed the card yourself (assign, comment, labels…): the revision your write printed | 5–30 |

Without an action, a term and a reason the ack is refused; the reference must exist. If the condition still holds at the check, the event comes back to you as pending, its age kept. Events by age (`blocked`, `in_progress_idle`, `review_waiting`, `inbox_waiting`, `overdue`) are one per card: a comment does not reset them. An event whose condition is gone resolves by itself; ack only what you handled.

| Rule | What to do |
|---|---|
| `blocked` | Read the card and its latest comments. Blocked by another agent or card — comment to that side and `kd-nudge` it. Needs the owner — put it into the report under «ждёт вас». |
| `dependency_done` | The dependencies are `Done`: `kd-boss-board unblock` with a reason; the board wakes the assignee itself. Refused (exit 2) — tell the owner, do not retry. |
| `in_progress_idle` | Comment «status?» on the card and `kd-nudge` the assignee. Level `escalate` — the owner already got a line from the platform; mention it in the report. |
| `review_waiting` | One reminder line to the owner for all such cards together. |
| `inbox_waiting` | Into the report under «ждёт вашего слова», with a recommendation: take / decline / to whom. |
| `due_tomorrow`, `due_today` | Comment the reminder on the card and `kd-nudge` the assignee. |
| `overdue` | One line to the owner with the card and who holds it. |
| `route_mismatch` | Assign by the project's route, `kd-task projects` (see `routing.md`). |
| `inbox_unassigned` | A new Inbox card nobody holds: read it and route it the same day (`routing.md` 2 and 5) — `assign` by the project's route, or `request-brief` when it cannot be started as written. A card that repeats another: `disputes.md`, «Duplicates». A handoff from an agent (label `from:<agent>`) is `coordinator_assigned`'s case below. |
| `spec_mismatch` | The card's `spec:` label is not one of its assignee's specializations (or there are two). `kd-boss-board specs` lists them per agent; set the right one together with the assignee — `assign --assignee @<agent> --spec <name>` — or drop it with `set-labels --remove spec:<x>` (an `assign` to another agent without `--spec` drops a label that agent does not hold by itself). |
| `coordinator_assigned` | The card is yours: read it and act — route it, split it (propose first) or answer the question on it. A handoff from an agent (label `from:<agent>`, in Inbox): `set-project` if its meaning needs another one, `assign` by the project's route (`@owner` only for what no agent can do; a `capability_missing` handoff goes to the project that holds the missing thing — `routing.md` 2), then `propose-promote` at once and tell the owner its number `KD-<n>` (no address), without waiting for the report — his tap on that decision in the cabinet is the decision on whether to start. This is not an escalation: no options message, the card itself carries the proposal. The owner does not take it (he says so, or it is still in Inbox after the decision expired) — use `comment` on each dependent card to say in the owner’s language that KD-<m> was declined and `kd-nudge` its assignee. |
| `brief_ready` | The analyst finished the brief (label `brief-ready`, card in Inbox on its implementer): `kd-boss-board revision`, then `propose-promote --reason "<brief ready and one line from the description, in the owner’s language>"`, tell the owner its number `KD-<n>` (no address). Do not touch the card after proposing (any edit — `set-labels` included — changes its revision and voids the proposal); the label stays. Changed card or a refusal — read the card; a brief without acceptance criteria goes back to `@analyst` with a comment. A card already out of Inbox — an S brief the cabinet started itself (KD-9249) — needs nothing. A brief its council still reads (the snapshot's `council round: brief <R>/<M> running` or `needs_writer`) raises no `brief_ready` and `propose-promote` is refused there («the brief's council has not ended»): propose nothing and do not hurry the analyst — the council's end wakes you with the brief to propose. |

Several events about one card: handle the card once, ack every event.

The tick runs in your «Монитор» thread — your work log: nobody is notified of what you write there, so end the tick with one short line of what you did. Every «tell the owner» above is a message in your chat: `kd-task chat --in-chat --post "<text>"`, one per tick at most, only what he must know or decide (CLAUDE.md, Duty 10). An event that needs nothing from him — you acted on it, or its condition is gone — gets nothing in the chat: the ack is in the journal. Before you tell him, read the chat (`kd-task chat --in-chat`): what he already decided or was already told is not repeated.

## Pipeline problems that are not attention events

These come as `[pipeline] …` signals, not as events of `attention-list`; there is nothing to ack. The card's snapshot (`kd-task view KD-<n>`) shows them on its `problem:` and `next (recorded)` lines, its stages on `stages:` and its release on `release:`.

| Signal | What to do |
|---|---|
| `stage_missing` — an approved stage has no child card | Read the parent. Tell the owner on the parent which stage (its key and title) has no card and that «Одобрить этапы» on the card creates it (the analyst re-plans with `kd-task edit --stages … --add-label brief-ready` first when the version must change). Never create the child yourself. |
| `stage_cancelled` — a stage's card was closed without the work | Read the parent and the closed child. One message to the owner on the parent: which stage, who closed it and why (its last messages), and your recommendation — reopen the child, a new version of the brief without it, or close the parent. |
| `stage_reopened` — a closed stage of the chain was reopened | A stage of a chain plan that was Done (you passed it, or the owner accepted it) is back in work, and every later stage builds on its commits. Read the parent (`stages:` line). A later stage in work (`In Progress`): `kd-nudge direct <agent> --card KD-<stage> --kind pause --message "<the earlier stage is reworked: wait for its new hand-in, in the owner’s language>"`; a stage not started yet waits by itself. One line to the owner on the parent: which stage was reopened and which later stages you paused. The stage's next Done closes the problem; then `kd-nudge direct … --kind work` to the paused ones, which take the reworked commits from the plan's branch. |
| `release_degraded` — the release of the card is degraded | The lead holds it. One comment on the card to the lead with the failed check (`release:` line: `check failed: …`) and the operation that failed, if any. The cabinet signals you again every 30 minutes while it stays degraded: the second time, if the lead has not answered, one line to the owner. A healthy check of the same revision or a later release closes the problem by itself. |
| `council_run_failed` — a critic of the council failed | One critic of the round did not read (its line in `kd-boss-board council --card KD-<n>` says why: no slot, a model without its key, a timeout, a result the cabinet could not keep). The round goes on with the others and the next round or the council's end closes the problem: nothing to do, unless the same critic fails round after round — then one line to the owner naming the model and the reason. |
| `council_failed` — no critic of the council could start | The council is over without a review and the work goes on without it. A brief: propose its start as any ready brief (`brief_ready`'s steps) and say in the reason that the council did not read it and why; a new handoff of the analyst gathers the council again. A pull request (`council round: code …` in `kd-task view KD-<n>`): its decision is open for the owner and says that nobody read — nothing to do; the next published head gathers the council again. The owner's notice already tells him. |
| `council_stalled` — the writer has not answered the council's findings | The writer of a brief is the analyst: the card is on it — or is an aggregate in work on its lead, which the analyst re-plans without holding. The writer of the code is the card's holder, the developer, and the owner's decision on its pull request stays closed until the council ends. Findings of the round have been unanswered for over an hour (the writer's question to the owner pauses that clock). Read `kd-task view KD-<n>` and the card's last messages: a failed or missing turn of the writer — `kd-nudge` it once with the card; still silent on your next look — one line to the owner. A council of the code on a card that you or a person hold has no writer at all — the row names that holder: a card of yours goes back to the developer that published the pull request, a person's — one line to the owner (return the card to the developer or cancel the council). Never answer a finding, edit the brief or touch the code yourself. |
| `council_unanswered` — the work was handed in past the findings | The analyst handed the brief in with `critical` or `major` findings of the round unanswered (the cabinet only notes it while the pipeline observes). Read `kd-boss-board council --card KD-<n>`: it lists the findings by their ids with the answers given so far. A changed brief is in its next round — nothing to do until it ends. The same brief handed in again ended the council with the findings open (`limited (unanswered)`) and `brief_ready` follows: propose the start as usual and name the open findings in the reason — the owner decides with them open. The problem closes when the work starts or a new council gathers. A developer that ended its turn for Review or Done past the findings of its pull request's council — unanswered, or answered `fixed` with no new head published — left the council waiting and the pull request's decision closed: `kd-nudge` it once with the card to answer the findings or publish the head; still so on your next look — one line to the owner, who may cancel the council. |
